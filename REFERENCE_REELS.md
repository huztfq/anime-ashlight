# Reference reels: what to copy (from Huzaifa, 2026-11-14)

Read this before writing reel hooks, captions, covers or Shorts titles, and in every three-way review (Claude + OpenAI + Grok Bot). Score each reel against the checklist below. Screenshots: refs/reels/.

## The references
1. https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfrEJGFTZ7J/ by @robertmckeaveney. 12.9K likes, 95 comments, 4.5K shares. Topic: "listen to your retention graph". The structure is HOOK, then a payoff that actually answers the question the hook raised.
2. https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfK4mPfNNPC/ by @danishmoosaa. 1.3K likes, 567 comments, 1.1K shares. The "story loop": make them care, make them curious, surprise them, then make them curious again (the rehook). CTA is "comment a word".
3. https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfhXDvnxhFX/ by @markpyvo. 903 likes, 1,436 comments, 1K shares. A provocative top-card line in second 0. On-screen CTA "Comment 'SFX' for links". It got more comments than likes.

## Rules for every AFTER THE HUM reel
1. **The hook lands in seconds 0-2.** Put one bold curiosity line on screen from the very first frame, for example as a top text card. Never open on a slow fade or an empty establishing shot.
2. **Story loop.** Care (a character in danger or wanting something), curious (a mystery), surprise (a reveal), then rehook (a new question at the end). The last shot should invite a rewatch or loop back.
3. **Pay off the hook.** The reel must answer or advance what the hook promised. Hook-only clips feel like bait and lose retention.
4. **The visual changes every 2-4 s.** That can be a cut, a push-in, a text card or an overlay. No static stretch longer than about 4 s.
5. **Text style.** Use bold white subtitles in short 2-5 word beats, centered low with a shadow, plus an optional top hook card. Text must never cover a face, and the frame stays full 9:16.
6. **Comment CTA (biggest driver of comments).** End with an on-screen line and a caption line asking for a one-word or theory comment, e.g. "Comment HUM if you heard it too" or "Broadcasting or listening? Comment your theory." Never promise a link or DM we can't deliver automatically.
7. **Short captions.** Use one punchy line, the question/CTA, "Full Episode on YouTube, link in bio", and #AfterTheHum plus up to 4 trending tags.
8. **Shares.** Aim for a moment worth sending to a friend, such as a twist, a theory or an eerie line, because shares were the strongest metric on all three references.

## Review scoring (each reviewer, 1-5 per item)
Hook in 0-2 s, story loop and rehook, payoff, visual change rate, text readability and placement, comment CTA, shareability. Fix anything that two reviewers score 2 or below before it posts.
